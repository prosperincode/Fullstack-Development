# The 3-Stage Trick Behind Every Addictive App

Original clipping: [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uoLpH_20qKw)  
Author: Tim Gabe  
Published: 2026-04-13  
Processed: 2026-05-10

## Summary

The clipping presents a three-stage reward sequence: anticipation before the result, ceremony during the reveal, and afterglow after the outcome. It argues that many apps flatten results into receipts when they could instead make important moments feel like gifts.

## Verified Updates

- This maps cleanly to product habit frameworks: trigger and action create the moment, variable reward and investment make it memorable and repeatable.
- The same pattern can be used ethically for meaningful milestones, progress reflection, education, health, and creativity, or unethically for manipulative overuse.
- Use ceremony proportionally. High-friction, high-risk domains such as investing, gambling-like rewards, and health decisions require more restraint than low-risk celebratory moments.
